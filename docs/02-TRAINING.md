# 02 — Training System

## Phase 1

**Name:** Foundation

**Length:** 12 weeks

**Schedule:**

- Monday — Upper Strength
- Tuesday — Lower Strength + Movement Foundation
- Wednesday — Upper Hypertrophy
- Thursday — Lower Hypertrophy
- Friday — Upper Specialization / Relative Strength Foundation
- Saturday — Long Easy Aerobic + Mobility
- Sunday — Complete Rest + Fast

### Primary objectives

1. Add visible muscle and improve proportions.
2. Improve bench strength.
3. Build strict pull-up and dip strength.
4. Build lower-body strength without forcing Olympic-lifting positions before mobility is ready.
5. Build an aerobic base from very low starting fitness.
6. Improve ankle, hip, thoracic, shoulder, front-rack, and split-stance prerequisites for eventual clean & jerk.
7. Prepare joints and tissues for later running.
8. Keep Sunday as complete rest and fasting day.

### Not included in Phase 1

- no swimming;
- no running;
- no HIIT;
- no clean & jerks;
- no power cleans;
- no muscle-up attempts;
- no hard cycling intervals.

---

# General lifting rules

## Logging

The user logs only:

- weight;
- reps.

The app does **not** require RIR logging.

RIR remains part of the prescription/coaching logic.

## Intensity

Default target:

- primary compounds: approximately 2 RIR;
- secondary compounds: approximately 1–2 RIR;
- isolations: approximately 1–2 RIR;
- movement-practice exercises: approximately 3–4 RIR / technically easy.

Never intentionally fail a compound lift.

## Rest

- primary compound: 3–4 minutes;
- ordinary compound: 2–3 minutes;
- isolation/accessory: 60–90 seconds;
- mobility/control: as needed.

## Progression model

Primary progression system: **double progression**.

Example:

Prescription: `4 × 4–6`

Stay at the same load until all prescribed working sets reach the top of the range with clean execution.

Example progression:

```text
185 × 6
185 × 5
185 × 5
185 × 4

then later

185 × 6
185 × 6
185 × 5
185 × 5

then

185 × 6
185 × 6
185 × 6
185 × 6
```

After `6/6/6/6`, increase by the exercise-specific increment next exposure.

The app should recommend the next load automatically.

### Load increments

Default:

- barbell upper-body compounds: +5 lb;
- dumbbell upper-body compounds: smallest practical pair increment;
- machine lower-body compounds: +10 lb when possible;
- leg press: +10 lb minimum, optionally +20 lb if equipment increments demand it;
- isolation movements: smallest practical increment;
- pull-ups: bodyweight until progression criteria are met, then add external load later.

If the gym equipment does not support the exact prescribed increment, round to the smallest available increment.

---

# Mesocycle effort pattern

Weeks 1–4:

- Week 1: establish loads, roughly 3 RIR compounds / 2–3 RIR isolation;
- Week 2: roughly 2 RIR;
- Week 3: roughly 1–2 RIR;
- Week 4: deload.

Weeks 5–8:

- Week 5: roughly 3 RIR;
- Week 6: roughly 2 RIR;
- Week 7: roughly 1–2 RIR;
- Week 8: deload.

Weeks 9–12:

- Week 9: roughly 2 RIR;
- Week 10: roughly 1–2 RIR;
- Week 11: hardest week, roughly 1 RIR on compounds and 0–1 RIR allowed on the last set of safe isolations;
- Week 12: deload + assessment.

## Deload rule

During deload:

- reduce working sets to approximately 50–60% of normal;
- keep movement patterns;
- use clearly submaximal loads;
- no grinders;
- cardio volume reduced roughly 30–40%.

# Monday — Upper Strength

## 1. Barbell Bench Press

- 4 sets
- 4–6 reps
- target ~2 RIR
- rest 3–4 min
- load increment: +5 lb after all 4 sets reach 6 reps

Purpose:

- primary bench-strength exposure;
- chest/triceps development;
- high specificity to bench goal.

## 2. Pull-Up / Assisted Pull-Up

- 4 × 5–8
- target ~2 RIR
- rest 2–3 min

Progression:

- if assisted, reduce assistance after all sets reach 8 clean reps;
- once bodyweight pull-ups are strong, use bodyweight progression;
- weighted pull-ups come in a later phase.

Purpose:

- lat and upper-back development;
- relative strength;
- future muscle-up prerequisite.

## 3. Incline Dumbbell Press

- 3 × 6–10
- target ~2 RIR
- rest 2–3 min

Purpose:

- upper-chest hypertrophy;
- secondary pressing volume.

## 4. Chest-Supported Row

- 3 × 6–10
- target ~2 RIR
- rest 2–3 min

Purpose:

- upper-back thickness;
- scapular strength;
- low lumbar fatigue.

## 5. Lateral Raise

- 3 × 12–20
- target 1–2 RIR
- rest 60–90 sec

## 6. Overhead Cable Triceps Extension

- 2 × 10–15
- target 1–2 RIR
- rest 60–90 sec

## 7. Incline Dumbbell Curl

- 2 × 10–15
- target 1–2 RIR
- rest 60–90 sec

### Monday PM Cardio

See cardio ramp below.

Preferred modality:

- stationary bike.

Effort:

- RPE 2–3;
- easy conversational effort.

# Tuesday — Lower Strength + Movement Foundation

## Movement prep

### Knee-to-wall ankle mobilization
- 1–2 × 8/side

### Supported deep squat
- 2 × 20–30 sec

### 90/90 hip switches
- 1 × 6/side

### Adductor rockback
- 1 × 8/side

## 1. Heel-Elevated Goblet Squat

- 2 × 8–10
- target 3–4 RIR
- rest ~90 sec

This is primarily movement practice, not the main strength stimulus.

Purpose:

- ankle/knee/hip coordination;
- deep squat familiarity;
- torso positioning.

## 2. Leg Press

- 4 × 5–8
- target ~2 RIR
- rest 3 min
- default increment +10 lb when all sets reach 8

Purpose:

- heavy quad strength/hypertrophy while mobility develops.

## 3. Romanian Deadlift

- 3 × 6–8
- target ~2 RIR
- rest 2–3 min

Purpose:

- hamstrings/glutes;
- hinge competency;
- posterior-chain foundation.

## 4. Reverse Lunge

- 2 × 8–10/leg
- target ~2 RIR
- rest 2 min

## 5. Seated Calf Raise

- 3 × 8–12
- target 1–2 RIR
- rest 90 sec

## 6. Tibialis Raise

- 2 × 12–20
- target 1–2 RIR
- rest 60 sec

## 7. Dead Bug

- 2 × 8–12/side
- controlled
- rest 60 sec

Purpose:

- trunk control;
- ribcage/pelvis control;
- useful foundation for later overhead work.

# Friday — Upper Specialization / Relative Strength Foundation

## Warm-up

### Scapular Pull-Up
- 2 × 5–8
- technically clean
- not taken near failure

## 1. Strict Pull-Up

- 3 × 5–8
- target ~2 RIR
- rest 2–3 min

If the user cannot yet perform the range strictly:

- use assistance;
- preserve strict form.

## 2. Dip / Assisted Dip

- 3 × 6–10
- target ~2 RIR
- rest 2–3 min

If shoulder position is uncomfortable:

- use assisted dip;
- do not force depth;
- choose a pain-free range.

# Saturday — Long Easy Aerobic + Mobility

Cardio should occur earlier in the day so there is time to eat, rehydrate, and recover before the Saturday-evening fast begins.

## Cardio

Preferred modality:

- stationary bike.

Secondary acceptable modality:

- incline treadmill walking once tolerated.

No intervals.

No threshold work.

No HIIT.

Target:

- RPE 2–3;
- conversational pace.

## Mobility checklist

### Knee-to-wall ankle mobilization
- 2 × 8/side

### Supported deep squat
- 2 × 30–45 sec

### 90/90 hip rotation
- 2 × 6/side

### Adductor rockback
- 2 × 8

### Thoracic extension
- 2 × 6 slow reps

### Bench lat / shoulder-flexion stretch
- 2 × 8

### Wall slide
- 2 × 8–10

### Stable split-stance hold
- 2 × 20–30 sec/side

Weeks 5–8 may add gentle PVC or empty-bar front-rack positional practice if comfortable.

Do not introduce cleans in Phase 1.

---

# Sunday — Complete Rest + Fast

Fast:

- Saturday evening to Sunday evening.

Sunday prescription:

- no lifting;
- no cardio;
- no recovery circuit;
- normal life walking is fine;
- optional gentle non-training mobility only if desired.

The app should display this as a true rest day.

---

# Cardio Ramp

The goal is to increase aerobic capacity without letting cardio meaningfully interfere with lifting or joint recovery.

## Weeks 1–2

- Monday: 15 min bike
- Wednesday: 20 min bike
- Friday: 15–20 min bike
- Saturday: 30 min bike

Approximate weekly total: 80–85 min.

## Weeks 3–4

- Monday: 20 min
- Tuesday: 15 min
- Wednesday: 20–25 min
- Friday: 20 min
- Saturday: 35–40 min

Week 4 is a deload: reduce total by ~30–40%.

## Weeks 5–8

AM lifting + PM cardio Monday through Friday.

Typical targets:

- Monday: 20–25 min
- Tuesday: 15–20 min
- Wednesday: 25–30 min
- Thursday: 15–20 min
- Friday: 25–30 min
- Saturday: 45–60 min

Week 8 is a deload: reduce total by ~30–40%.

## Weeks 9–11

- Monday: 25–30 min
- Tuesday: 20–25 min
- Wednesday: 30 min
- Thursday: 20–25 min
- Friday: 30 min
- Saturday: 60–75 min

## Week 12

Deload.

Reduce total cardio volume approximately 30–40%.

# Cardio progression behavior in app

Cardio targets should be stored week-by-week in program data rather than inferred from lifting logic.

The app should display the scheduled duration automatically.

RPE is logged only after completion.

---

# Future-phase readiness

Phase 1 should produce evidence that the user is ready for more specific training.

Desired outcomes by end of Phase 1:

- 60–75 min easy cycling feels manageable;
- improved strict pull-up performance;
- improved dip performance;
- bench strength trending upward;
- better deep-squat control;
- improved ankle mobility;
- improved overhead position;
- better hinge mechanics;
- stable split stance;
- improved general work capacity.

Phase 2 may begin introducing:

- run/walk;
- swimming;
- Olympic-lifting derivatives;
- more specific front-rack work;
- muscle-up-specific skill work.

Those are **not** part of V1 Phase 1 execution.
